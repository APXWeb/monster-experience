> Inferred from the user's written brief (2026-10-12). The user asked to proceed without confirmation rounds, so every fact below comes from that brief or from the official Monster Energy Brazil site; nothing was confirmed in an interview.

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + React + TypeScript, React Three Fiber (three.js) for the 3D stage, GSAP ScrollTrigger for scroll-linked choreography, Lenis for smooth scroll. Same toolchain as APX Web's NOIR X1 concept, already proven on this machine and on GitHub Pages. Static build, no backend.

## Users

Prospective APX Web clients (brand, marketing and product teams) evaluating whether the studio can deliver premium immersive web work. They arrive from the APX portfolio, usually on desktop, sometimes on a phone, and judge in the first viewport and the first few scrolls.

Secondary: anyone curious about the Monster Energy Brazil range who wants to browse the variants and their official label figures.

## Product Purpose

A conceptual, independent web experience built on the Monster Energy brand as visual and product reference. It proves APX Web's capacity in 3D, motion design and scroll storytelling: a scroll-controlled cinematic journey (origin, formulation, can forming, identity, filling, reveal) that turns into an interactive catalog of every variant listed on the official Brazilian catalog.

Success: a visitor keeps scrolling to see what happens next, then explores flavors in detail, and leaves convinced APX can build this level of work.

## Positioning

Not an official site. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Monster Energy. The journey is a stylized interpretation of how an industrial product comes to be; it never claims to show Monster's real formula, factory, or quality controls.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Scroll drives the narrative: reversible, predictable, pinned only where it helps.
- Catalog of all variants on monsterenergy.com/pt-br at research time (2026-10-12): 17 entries across Monster Energy, Monster Ultra, Dragon Tea, Juice Monster. Line filters, per-variant detail with prev/next and return.
- Per-variant data only from official pages; missing fields stay absent and are labeled as not informed. Every record keeps its source URL.
- No purchase or support buttons that imitate official channels.
- Fallback when WebGL is missing; reduced-motion variant; keyboard access.

## Brand Commitments

- Monster Energy marks (claw logo, can art) are used as reference assets from the official site, never redrawn.
- Required disclaimer: "Projeto conceitual desenvolvido pela APX Web. Não afiliado ou endossado pela Monster Energy."
- APX copy conventions: no em dashes in visible text; never invent facts about real brands.
- User taste (from past projects): no thin or monospace fonts, no grid or blueprint backgrounds.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official product pages (pt-BR) with name, flavor line, description, calories, caffeine, total sugars, product render.
- Two user-supplied reference videos (Downloads, 2026-10-12): floating 3D product carousel; scroll-driven logistics story. Quality references only, not to be copied.
- Absent and not to be fabricated: full nutrition tables, ingredient lists and volumes where the official page does not publish them; any testimonial, factory fact or corporate data.

## Product Principles

1. The scroll is the director: every gesture advances or rewinds one continuous scene.
2. The can is a physical object, never a flat image pretending.
3. Official data or nothing.
4. One experience from first frame to last flavor; no seam between story and catalog.
5. Spectacle never costs fluidity; limited devices get a designed fallback, not a broken copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for text, visible focus, full keyboard path through filters and detail views, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored, no flashing.
