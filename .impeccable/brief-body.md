# Surface brief: index (single-page experience)

Scope: the whole one-page experience (narrative + catalog + close). Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience: prospective APX Web clients judging studio capability; secondary, curious visitors browsing the Brazilian Monster range.
Job: follow the scroll-directed journey, then explore every variant with its official figures.

Constraints: concept disclaimer, no purchase/support imitation, no invented data, reduced-motion and no-WebGL fallbacks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a six-station press line the scroll feeds one can through; it refuses the category default of a can spinning beside text blocks. Every station transforms the object itself (droplets fuse, vessel fills, a disc is drawn into a can, the label is printed, the can is filled and seamed, the light reveals it) and the line's output becomes the flavor rack.

OWN-WORLD: Press-shop dark: black ink and graphite steel ground, acid green as the line's working light, raw aluminium as the material voice. Heavy industrial condensed caps (Big Shoulders Display 800/900) set huge and tight, Figtree for reading. Station placards with a numeral and a short heavy title; figures read as calibrated instruments on one shared scale. Each product line owns its color by law once the rack opens (raise from the arcade challenger: palette discipline as rule, not decoration).

STORY: The visitor sees the can as a physical object, scrolls to rewind it to a single drop, watches it become metal, identity and product, then picks up the rack to compare all 17 Brazilian variants and open any one in detail.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL. Original Green can at ~72vh height, right of center, tilted 8 degrees, condensation on the metal, green volumetric haze rising from below. Left third: the headline in heavy condensed caps at ~9vw, two lines. Top bar: APX Web concept mark left, station rail 00/06 right. Bottom left: scroll cue with a live progress bar. No CTA button: the scroll is the action.

FORM: Press-line stations (die stations, struck in sequence) with the can-making progression as its literal topology; position 6 of my ordered list; seed key 0dd0ac5b. Raises: night instrument six-pack (figures on a shared calibrated scale so variants cross-check); forging challenger (forming advances in discrete strikes with registered measures); fashion mook (can anatomy callouts during forming); highlife sleeve (display type packed huge, cheek to cheek).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: scroll-scrubbed lathe morph, a flat aluminium disc drawn into a necked can by a punch, reversible frame by frame.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
